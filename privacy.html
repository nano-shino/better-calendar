Privacy

Better Calendar · last updated 16 November 2026

Better Calendar shows your Google Calendar events as a list of photo cards. This page explains what it reads from your Google account, where that goes, and how to remove it.

What it reads from Google

  • Your calendars and events, read-only (calendar.readonly). It never creates, changes or deletes anything in your calendar.
  • Your name, email address and profile picture, to show which account each calendar comes from.

Where your data goes

  • Calendar events stay in your browser. Your browser fetches them directly from Google, keeps them in memory while the page is open, and never sends them to any server of ours. So that the page opens faster, the list of your calendars (their names and colors, no events) is remembered in this browser until you sign out or remove the account.
  • Settings sync through Google Firebase, so they follow you between devices: the Google accounts you linked (name, email, picture), which calendars you hid, the photos you chose for events, photos you uploaded, and which events you marked as done (their ID and start time, not their details). Only you can read them.
  • Sign-in tokens. Short-lived access tokens are kept in your browser. To keep your accounts connected without signing in every hour, a small token server (on Cloudflare) stores one refresh token per linked account, encrypted. It uses them only to get new access tokens for you, and never reads your calendar.
  • Photo search. When you search for a photo, the words in the search box (which may start from the event's title) are sent to Openverse to find Creative Commons images.

Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API data

Better Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to show you your own calendar in this app.

Removing your data

  • Remove an account from the avatar menu. Its stored refresh token is revoked at Google and deleted.
  • Revoke access at any time from your Google account's third-party connections.
  • Delete your saved settings and photos by asking through the contact link below.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub.
